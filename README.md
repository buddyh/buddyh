# Buddy Hadry

I build around the rough edges of my own workflow: sessions, context, integrations. And the occasional touch of madness.

[buddyhadry.com](https://buddyhadry.com) · [@buddyhadry on X](https://x.com/buddyhadry)

## Open source

- **[seshy](https://github.com/buddyh/seshy)** — Find and resume coding-agent sessions across tools.
- **[Fieldpacket](https://github.com/buddyh/fieldpacket)** — Turn a research brief into a sourced, shareable report.
- **[alexa-cli](https://github.com/buddyh/alexa-cli)** — Control Alexa devices from the terminal or an agent.
- **[Todoist CLI](https://github.com/buddyh/todoist-cli)** — Manage tasks and projects from the command line.
- **[agent-skills](https://github.com/buddyh/agent-skills)** — Skills built for my own agent workflows.
- **[Agent Radio](https://github.com/buddyh/agent-radio)** — Listen to your agents through a continuous audio stream.
- **[Screen Narrator](https://github.com/buddyh/narrator)** — Give your screen a sportscaster, noir narrator, or another voice.

## Other apps

- **[Sidebridge](https://sidebridge.app)** — A native macOS sidebar for terminal and coding-agent context.
- **[Proceduria](https://proceduria.com)** — Make procedural wallpapers in your browser.
